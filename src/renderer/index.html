Settings

Playback

Standard uses lower bitrate audio for faster loading. Plus uses regular quality. Changes apply to the next track.

Off

Overlap tracks by up to 12 seconds when the next track is ready. Set to 0 to turn it off.

My Wave

My Wave picks a song at random from your recent plays and liked songs, then builds a queue of related tracks from the music catalog. If recommendations are unavailable, it plays songs from your recent plays and likes.

Import a playlist

Paste a public playlist link from Spotify, Yandex Music, or VK Music. HeadRoom matches the songs and saves them in a new playlist.
